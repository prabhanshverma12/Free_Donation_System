How It Works

Three simple steps to share items and reduce waste.

1

Post or Request

Donors list extra food, books, clothes or electronics with details. People in need send a items request.

2

Get Matched

Browse available donations or open requests and connect directly with the person nearby.

3

Share the Items

Mark the donation or request as fulfilled once the item is handed over. Nothing wasted.
